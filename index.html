It is a target paper released shortly before the board exam, often around midnight, to help students revise the most important topics and question patterns in their final hours. When is it released? Midnight papers are usually released 1 to 2 days before the board paper, and at most one week before. The exact date and time are announced on our WhatsApp channel. What is the price? This is a paid product. The price is announced on our WhatsApp channel before the paper, so you can decide with full information. Is it exactly the same as the paper? Midnight papers are prepared to match the board pattern and often contain many closely matching questions. However, no paper can be guaranteed to match exactly.
